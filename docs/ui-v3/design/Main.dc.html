Walk in and you're in a simulator bay: the club is lit like the brand's product shots, on black. The fitter talks to you in daylight. Brand ratio 60 / 30 / 10 holds: black stage, white conversation, orange for you.

  1. 01The stage is the bay. Midnight black, a faint range grid, the club hero-lit. Never pure #000.
  2. 02One question at a time. The fitter speaks in plain text. Every closed question is a component, never a form page.
  3. 03Orange means you. Your fit, your measured shots, the next action. Alternatives are white and grey.
  4. 04Solid is measured, hollow is a guess. Every number carries where it came from.
  5. 05Show the working. One-line why per spec. Tap for the numbers. Close calls said out loud.
