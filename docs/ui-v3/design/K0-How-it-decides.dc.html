How the chat decides

One question at a time, in a logical order

The fitter asks like a person at the range: who you are, how you score, how you swing, what you want, what hurts, then your body. Every step is one card. Anything the golfer already told us, or that data already shows, is skipped.

Five rules
  1. 1One question per turnNever more than one input on screen. Each card asks one thing, so no step asks too much.
  2. 2Ask only what data can't tell usImports come first, questions fill the gaps. Body measurements come last, when the golfer is invested.
  3. 3Every closed question is tappableYou can finish the whole fitting without typing. Typing and voice always work too.
  4. 4Follow how they answerTappers get full cards. Typers get text-first replies with small inline chips. Talkers get a live transcript and the same chips.
  5. 5Stop at enough confidenceOnce the fit is confident, show it. More data becomes an optional way to sharpen it, never a gate.
Three ways to answer, switched automatically
Tap modeDefault. They tap cards: next card is a full component.
