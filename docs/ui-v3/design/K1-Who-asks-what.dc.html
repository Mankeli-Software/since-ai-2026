Who asks what

Different golfers, different questions

Questions the fitter should expect after the result, by level, and the component that answers each one best. Personas from the participant pack. The depth of every answer follows the level.

Starting outAino 29, Emma 33, Marja 66Wants direction and reassurance, not spec sheets. Plain words, one recommendation, true social proof.
"Which irons should I even get?"→recommendation_cardOne head, one sentence why
"What is flex? Do I need to care?"→explainerA picture, no numbers
"Will these stop my slice?"→landing_ringsYour spread now vs with this club
"Do I need all these clubs?"→set_builderWhich irons you will really use
"Can I send them back?"→stock_vs_custom30-day returns badge
Getting seriousMikko 46, Kaisa 48, Tom 35, Pekka 74Wants the reason for each spec and to compare two options. Numbers on request.
"Why regular and not stiff?"→flex_bandYour speed in the flex bands
"201 or 201T for me?"→club_compare + flight_compareSwap the head, see it fly
"How far will my 7-iron go?"→flight_compareCarry, apex, landing
"My knuckles ache"→pain_checkGrip size and graphite answer
"I guessed my glove size"→body_measure (glove)Hand drawing, size tiles
Low handicapLars 38, Hannu 60, Anders 44, Ben 31, Noora 24Wants evidence that confirms what they suspect. Real numbers, honest trade-offs.
"CB or MB? Talk me through it"→close_callTrade-off, not a winner
"Does my lie really need to change?"→strike_map + lie_testStrikes and ball-line marks
"Show me spin and landing angle"→numbers_tablePer head, from your shots
"+1° custom: worth losing the return?"→stock_vs_customStrikes and start line, side by side
"Gap my lofts against my set"→set_gappingCarry gaps 5 to PW
