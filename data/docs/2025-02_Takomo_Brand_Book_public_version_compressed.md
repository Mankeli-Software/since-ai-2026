Takomo operates against this grain. We know clubs don't fix a bad swing, and we don't tell our buyers that they will. We're honest about our products, the industry, and who we are. In an industry overrun with marketing BS, Takomo is the honest golf company.

### Slide 9: Market positioning

# A BRAND THAT STANDS OUT.

Through staying within the Takomo success Zone, we strike a balance between the two buying factors that our customers most care about while also occupying an unoccupied niche in the market.

The money zone is the goal. Direct to consumer price, and perceived quality between Taylormade and Titleist. An interiely unoccupied zone in the market.

To understand Takomo's brand positioning, it's important to understand how our competition positions themselves and how they communicate. By and large, there are two ends of the industry:

The old brands (mostly retail): Tech/innovation focused, tour-focused, "The number one X in golf."

The new brands (mostly D2C): Premium X at low prices, budget, everyman/party golf.

### Slide 11: Brand character: Brand archetype

**Sage**

A Sage person has a desire not only to understand the world, but also to share that understanding with others. They are thought leaders and reliable information providers. They believe there is no limit to what they can learn and are constantly educating themselves.

Sage brands are aimed towards people who are intelligent and place a high value on quality. The Sage's core desire is the discovery of the truth, and they fear ignorance and dislike misleading information.

**Jester**

The Jester archetype values humor, fun, living in the moment, and not taking things too seriously. Core traits include being playful, irreverent, witty, and socially confident. The Jester archetype embodies humor, spontaneity, and an infectious zest for life. Symbolic of common sense and of honesty.

The Jester enjoy attracting attention and encouraging others to laugh along with them. They are young at heart, and they never seem to mature or become serious. Jester brands aim to promote good times and make their target audience laugh, and they are afraid of boredom or loneliness.

*Illustrated with black-and-white film stills: Yoda for the Sage, Jim Carrey as Ace Ventura for the Jester.*

### Slide 14: Tone of voice

**EVERYMAN OVER AUTHORITY**
Takomo communicates in the same language used by everyday golfers, not academic authorities. We know what we're talking about and can go deep, but we do it using normal, relatable ways. We're your friend, not a textbook.

**FUNNY OVER PLAIN, BORING, OR CHEESY**
People don't trust brands. People don't like ads. The ultimate antidote to this is humor. Takomo is funny and witty. We avoid boring communication. Cringey, cliché, cheesy, or outdated humor is to be avoided. We aim to instill customer delight with our humor, not outrage.

**CANDID OVER FORMAL**
Takomo talks to its customers and prospects as if we're having a one-on-one, candid conversation with a friend. We're honest, we're relatable, we talk the way they talk. Avoid overly formal or precise language. Takomo fans don't want to talk to ChatGPT. They want to talk to a friend.

**TECHNICAL OVER REDUCTIVE**
When it's time to talk tech, we do so simply, clearly, and honestly. Avoid the oversimplification, wool-over-the-eyes, marketing BS the rest of the industry loves. We are fun and relatable, but we're also golf equipment experts.

*Background: illustration of an armoured figure hauling an ore cart full of dark crystals up a hillside.*

## Visual language

*Section divider (slide 16): "VISUAL LANGUAGE".*

### Slide 17: Visual language

# MODERN AND FREE

We live in an era where everything is fluid, free and expressive. We don't want to put our brand into a rigid mold in which we can't move and be free. Be imaginative, push your creativity and keep being bold and interesting.

However there are three pillars that guide our visual language.

**THE PRODUCT**
Since we are pushing the boundaries between high quality and affordability it is important that our products are shown in a premium way. No exceptions.

**THE IMAGERY**
When it comes to golf and the lifestyle around it we are definitely rough around the edges. Anything that looks professional golf with perfect green fairways and blue sky is not us. We keep it real.

**THE CONTENT**
We love to push our boundaries and come up with unique and new ways to showcase our brand and products. Worry only about creativity.

*Three example images: an iron head on a black background, an aerial shot of a crowd around a green, and the blacksmith illustration.*

### Slide 18: Logo design

# THE TAKOMO LOGO

**SIMPLICITY**
The logo mark is a T shape created from a simple golf swing pattern. The sharpened edges gives is a handcrafted and forged feel.

**SCANDINAVIAN**
The wordmark (brand name) is minimalistic and draws inspiration from Scandinavian design brands. It has spacing between each letter to give it room to breath and some extra width and stability.

**CRAFTMANSHIP**
To make the typography more unique and give it that handcrafted feel the edges of some chararacters have been sharpened.

*Shows the full lockup: the T mark followed by the widely spaced wordmark "TAKOMO", in black on washed white.*

### Slide 19: Typography

# THE BARLOW FAMILY

Barlow is a slightly rounded, low-contrast, grotesk type family. The font superfamily has a Regular, Semi Condensed and Condensed family, each with 9 weights in Roman and Italic.

For headlines and bigger text our chosen font is Barlow Condensed. This is frequently used across marketing material and product design.

For body text and longer captions we are using Barlow. It's imortant to use Barlow when the text needs to be readable, especially on smaller touchpoints.

### Slide 20: Colour

# COLOUR PROFILE

Our primary colours are black, white and orange. We stay away from a full black colour.

The ratio of our main colours are 60/30/10. In general we want to push black and darker shades to create a clear look & feel for our brand and move away from a "generic white" feel.

Monochrome styles is something we like and you can see the simple style we use for example on our new driver. Black on black with different applications and orange as an accent colour.
