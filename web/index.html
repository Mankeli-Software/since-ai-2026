OUR STORY

Golf, The Fair Way

Premium golf clubs without premium price tags

Here’s the thing: traditional golf brands run on heavy business models. The result? Everyday golfers practically have to rob a bank to afford high-performance clubs.

Fed up with the overpricing, Takomo was founded to do things differently. We deliver premium quality comparable to the big boys. But thanks to our direct-to-consumer model, our clubs are priced much more accessibly.

Premium golf clubs with a price tag that actually makes sense — That's Takomo!
